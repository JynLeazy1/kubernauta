What is a Service?
Virtual IPs, kube-proxy, and the Path of a Packet
A Service is not a process or a network interface. It is a virtual IP the kernel intercepts through iptables or IPVS rules. We analyze kube-proxy, EndpointSlices, proxy modes, and trace the complete path of a packet from a Pod to its destination.

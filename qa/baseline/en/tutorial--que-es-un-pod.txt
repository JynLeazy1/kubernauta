What is a Pod?
Kubernetes' Smallest Unit from the Inside Out
A Pod is not a container. It is an abstraction that groups one or more containers sharing network and storage namespaces. We dissect its anatomy, the pause container, the lifecycle, and how kubelet actually brings it up.
